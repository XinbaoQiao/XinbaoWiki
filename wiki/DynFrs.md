**DynFrs** is an ICLR 2025 paper on efficient machine unlearning for random forests.

## Idea

The method combines occurrence control, lazy tag strategies, and tree-ensemble structure to support efficient updates after deletion requests. It targets privacy-sensitive domains where random forests remain common, including healthcare, finance, and recommender systems.
