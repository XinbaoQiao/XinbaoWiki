**AI and Networks** covers AI for communication, communication for AI, decentralized learning, and cloud-edge collaborative systems.

## Directions

- Efficient decentralized learning and data pruning.
- Communication-aware machine learning.
- Cloud-edge collaborative healthcare using multimodal monitoring.
- Distributed synthetic-data evaluation and Wasserstein-based collaboration.
