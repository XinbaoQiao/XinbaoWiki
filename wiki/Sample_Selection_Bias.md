---
name: "Sample Selection Bias"
occupation: "Research topic"
summary: "Bias induced by non-representative sample selection."
---

**Sample Selection Bias** occurs when selected samples are not representative of the target distribution. In data silos, a verifier may select synthetic samples that match only a local view of the world.

This mechanism is central to [[When_Sample_Selection_Bias_Precipitates_Model_Collapse]].
